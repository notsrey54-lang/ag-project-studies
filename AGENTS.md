## TaskPlanner workflow

- Read `.tasks/config.json` before task operations.
- Select the highest-priority task from Next, then Backlog, and move it to In Progress before substantive implementation.
- Keep the task's `### Plan` concise and current while working.
- After verification, condense the plan to the completed outcome, move the task to Done, and add a short entry to `.tasks/WORK_LOG.md`.
- Preserve unrelated task sections and do not change task IDs.
